Water-Fish | One web, one world, one flow


WaterFish is a lightweight very barebones web browser made in python

Unfortunately it only minimally or doesn't even support css/js which means most modern pages won't open

This project is not anything serious I was just playing around with the idea of making a browser after recent privacy discussions around major browsers
